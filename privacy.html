Privacy

There is nothing to collect, so nothing is collected.

Impromptu has no account system, no database and no analytics. Everything you write stays in your own browser. This page sets out exactly what is stored, what leaves your device, and how to delete it.

Effective 27 July 2026 · Version 1.0.0

The short version. No sign-up, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party trackers. Your notes never leave your device. Two outbound requests exist — a font stylesheet and a definition lookup — and neither carries anything you wrote.

What is stored on your device

Impromptu writes a single entry to your browser's localStorage under the key impromptu:v1. It holds:

  • Your theme choice, difficulty filter and selected fields
  • Your topic history and saved topics
  • The notes you write, and which research checklist items you have ticked
  • Cached definitions for topics you have already drawn

This sits on your machine only. It is not transmitted, backed up or synced — which also means it is not recoverable if you clear your browser data or move to another device.

Deleting it

Clear site data for this domain in your browser settings, or open developer tools and run localStorage.removeItem("impromptu:v1"). A private or incognito window leaves nothing behind at all. There is no server-side copy to request, export or erase, because there is no server.

What leaves your device

Definition lookups

When a topic is drawn, the app requests a one-sentence summary from the Wikipedia API, operated by the Wikimedia Foundation. That request carries the topic name and — unavoidably, as with any web request — your IP address and browser user-agent. It carries nothing you have written. Results are cached locally, so drawing the same topic again makes no request at all. Wikimedia's handling is governed by the Wikimedia Privacy Policy.

Fonts

Inter and JetBrains Mono load from Google Fonts, which exposes your IP address and user-agent to Google. See the Google Privacy Policy. If you would rather avoid it, the fonts can be self-hosted or removed by deleting three tags in the page head — the app works without them.

Hosting

Whoever serves these files keeps standard access logs, typically IP address, timestamp, requested path and user-agent. That is ordinary infrastructure logging, not analytics, and it is not connected to anything you do inside the app.

What is never collected

  • Names, email addresses or account details — none are ever requested
  • The content of your notes or explanations
  • Audio. Nothing is recorded; the presentation timer is a countdown and nothing more
  • Behavioural or advertising profiles

Cookies

Impromptu sets no cookies of any kind. localStorage is used instead, it is never sent to a server, and no consent banner is required because there is nothing to consent to.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA grant rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal data. Those rights are satisfied here by design: no personal data is collected or held, and the only data that exists is already in your hands and deletable by you at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and the edit is visible in the project's commit history on GitHub.
